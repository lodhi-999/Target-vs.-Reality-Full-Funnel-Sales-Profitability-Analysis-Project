# Target-vs.-Reality-Full-Funnel-Sales-Profitability-Analysis-Project
Analyzes sales and target data across categories and regions to identify top performers, losses, and trends. Offers actionable insights on improving profitability, meeting targets, and enhancing operational efficiency using data-driven visuals and analysis.


# 🧠 Comprehensive Sales Performance & Target Achievement Analysis

This project delivers an in-depth analysis of sales, profit trends, and target achievements using transactional and performance datasets. The aim is to uncover actionable insights across product categories, regional performance, and time-based trends to drive better decision-making.

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The analysis is based on three datasets:
- **List_of_Orders.csv** – Contains customer orders and metadata.
- **Order_Details.csv** – Includes item-level sales, profit/loss, and category details.
- **Sales_Target.csv** – Monthly target data for various categories.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and preprocess order and sales data.
- Identify top and underperforming categories.
- Analyze sales performance by state and product category.
- Compare actual sales vs. monthly targets (esp. Furniture category).
- Highlight month-over-month changes and regional disparities.
- Suggest actionable improvements for low-performing segments.

---

## 🧰 Technologies Used

- **Python**
- **Pandas, NumPy** – Data wrangling and transformation.
- **Matplotlib, Seaborn** – Visualization.
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis and reporting environment.

---

## 📌 Observations

- **Clothing** has consistent sales with high total profit despite lower average profit per order.
- **Furniture** has the lowest overall performance; deeper intervention required.
- **Electronics** generates strong sales and profit—suggesting room for margin optimization.
- **MP & Maharashtra** lead in sales and order volumes; **Delhi** shows strong profit margins.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Offer more discounts on best-selling Clothing items (e.g., Sarees, Trousers).
- Run surveys and introduce customization options to revive Furniture sales.
- Improve logistics and expand advertising in low-performing states (e.g., Gujarat).
- Align inventory with seasonal demand and target fluctuations.
